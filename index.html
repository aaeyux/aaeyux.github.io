The Artist

Frames that tell
stories

I'm Saksham — a visual storyteller based between the mountains and the city. With over 8 years behind the lens, I've developed an eye for the extraordinary in the everyday: the way golden hour transforms a street, the split second a subject's guard drops, the rhythm of a city at night.

My work spans across commercial photography, narrative short films, brand campaigns, wedding cinematography, and documentary work. Every project is approached with the same obsession — to make the viewer feel something.
